# Lakshya's Birthday Photo Gallery Directory

Drop your personal photographs in this folder to personalize the website!

## Required Image Files:
1. `hero.jpg` - Main cinematic hero photograph of Lakshya
2. `lakshya.jpg` - Dedicated portrait for the "Her Name" section
3. `couple-01.jpg` - The first memory / early days
4. `couple-02.jpg` - Mid-story romantic moment (sunset, coffee date)
5. `couple-03.jpg` - Recent favorite photograph of both of you
6. `memory-01.jpg` - Gallery photo 1 (Late night talk / golden hour)
7. `memory-02.jpg` - Gallery photo 2 (Her candid laughter)
8. `memory-03.jpg` - Gallery photo 3 (Special trip / road trip)
9. `memory-04.jpg` - Gallery photo 4 (Cozy morning / rainy day)
10. `memory-05.jpg` - Gallery photo 5 (Celebration / dressed up night)
11. `memory-06.jpg` - Gallery photo 6 (Pure, candid smile)

### Tips for Best Quality:
- Vertical portraits (3:4 or 4:5 ratio) work best for `hero.jpg`, `lakshya.jpg`, and `memory-01` to `06`.
- Horizontal landscape (16:9 or 4:3) works great for `couple-01`, `couple-02`, and `couple-03`.
- Any resolution from 1080px to 4K will be automatically optimized and gracefully cropped by the website.
- While this folder is empty or before you add your photos, the website automatically displays beautiful romantic editorial placeholders with zero broken images.
